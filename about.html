Who's Behind GambleClear, and How We Research

Here's what this site is, how it's put together, and where the information on it comes from.

Who writes GambleClear

GambleClear is an independent project covering crypto casinos, built for players who want the fees, currencies, and game mechanics laid out clearly before they sign up anywhere. It isn't owned or funded by any casino operator.

How we research each platform

Our coverage is compiled from each operator's own published terms and help documentation, publicly available policy pages, and how the underlying systems (provably fair verification, blockchain network confirmation times, KYC thresholds) generally work. We update pages when bonus terms, fees, or policies change materially.

Our rating methodology

Where we score or rank a casino, we weigh five factors, consistently applied across every platform we cover:

FactorWeightWhat we look at
KYC friction25%How much identity verification is required to deposit, play, and withdraw at normal amounts
Provable fairness & trust20%Whether games are independently verifiable, and how transparent the operator is about odds/house edge
Crypto support & speed20%Breadth of supported currencies and typical network-level deposit/withdrawal speed
Game variety20%Depth of in-house originals plus breadth of slots/live-dealer library
Licensing15%Jurisdiction held and what player protections that license does or doesn't require

Scores built from this methodology reflect published terms and documentation, not hands-on play-testing; see the note on each review page for what that specific page is based on. We publish the criteria openly so you can weigh them differently than we do if your priorities differ (for example, weighting licensing higher if regulatory protection matters more to you than KYC friction).
