Rakeback vs. a deposit bonus

A deposit bonus adds bonus funds on top of what you put in, usually with wagering requirements before anything is withdrawable. Rakeback works on the other side of the transaction: it returns a slice of the house edge you're already generating through normal play, credited as you go rather than locked behind a rollover target. It's a smaller number on paper than a "100% match" headline, but it's real, unlocked value rather than funds tied up in fine print.

The VIP ladder, in plain terms

This is the mechanism that determines your long-run rakeback rate once the welcome period ends, so frequent players end up on better ongoing terms than the headline welcome number alone suggests.
