Responsible Gambling

Gambling Should Stay Entertainment, Not a Problem

Everything on this site assumes gambling is a form of paid entertainment with a built-in house edge, not a way to make money. If it stops feeling like entertainment, that's worth taking seriously.

Age requirement

Every casino reviewed on this site requires players to be 18 or older, and 21 or older in jurisdictions where that's the legal minimum. If you are under the applicable legal age, please leave this site.

Signs it's becoming a problem

Chasing losses with bigger bets, gambling with money set aside for essentials, hiding the extent of your play from people close to you, or feeling unable to stop even when you want to are all signs worth paying attention to, not ignoring.

Tools that can help

Most operators offer deposit limits, loss limits, cool-off periods, and full self-exclusion directly in account settings. Set these before you think you need them, not after. Check your account's settings menu or help center for the current options.

Support organizations

If gambling is affecting your life or someone else's, these organizations offer confidential support regardless of where you're located: Gambling Therapy (international, multilingual online support) and GamCare's international support directory, which links out to national helplines by country. Players in the Philippines and wider Asia can also reach GamblingHelp Asia.
